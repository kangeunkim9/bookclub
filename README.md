# Korean Literature Book Club

A reading record of the Korean Literature Book Club at Sungkyunkwan University.

Exchange students and Korean students meet every two weeks and read Korean fiction in English translation. After each meeting everyone writes a short commentary, the way you would leave a note on a film you have just seen. This site collects them.

**[kangeunkim9.github.io/bookclub](https://kangeunkim9.github.io/bookclub/)**

Books are chosen by the members themselves. At the first meeting of the semester everyone brings one title, and the club picks four out of them.

Readers so far have come from Austria, China, the Czech Republic, Germany, Hungary, Korea, Slovakia and the United Kingdom.

## About the reviews

Reviews appear as they were written, in whatever language the reader chose, and nothing is shortened. English translations of Korean reviews are by Claude. Readers are identified only by country and university.

## How the site is built

One `index.html`, no build step, served by GitHub Pages. Each book is a view inside that page, and its reviews live in the script at the bottom as a plain array. Adding a meeting means adding one array of reviews and one view.
