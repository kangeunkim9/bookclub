Korean Literature Book Club

Tuesdays 6 PM, every two weeks · SKKU Humanities & Social Sciences Campus

Korean and international students read Korean novels and short stories in English translation and talk about them freely. We started in Fall 2025.

How we read

  • At the first meeting everyone brings one book they would like to read, and we choose four titles for the semester.
  • At each meeting one member gives a short summary, and then we all share our thoughts.
  • Everyone writes a short commentary like a film review. At the end of the semester we collect them as a record of the club.

Who we are

Korean students and exchange students at SKKU. This semester our readers come from Austria, the Czech Republic, Germany, Hungary, Korea, Slovakia and the UK.

No Korean needed. We read everything in English translation.
